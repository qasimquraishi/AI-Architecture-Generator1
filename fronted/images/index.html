                        ABOUT ARCHI GENERATOR
                    

                    
Architecture Powered by AI


                    

                        Archi Generator combines architectural thinking
                        with modern artificial intelligence.
                    


                



                


                    


                        

                    


                    


                        

                            Turning Ideas Into Architectural Concepts
                        


                        

                            Our platform helps users transform property
                            requirements into structured preliminary
                            architectural concepts.
                        


                        

                            Users can provide plot size, floors, bedrooms,
                            bathrooms, parking requirements and architectural
                            style.
                        


                        

                            The AI then prepares a professional concept
                            that can be reviewed and refined by an
                            architect.
                        


                        


                            
                                Professional Review Required
                            

                            

                                AI-generated concepts are preliminary
                                and must be reviewed by a qualified
                                architect or structural engineer before
                                construction.
