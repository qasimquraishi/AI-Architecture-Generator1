About Archi Generator

Archi Generator is an AI-powered architectural concept platform designed to help clients, designers and teams turn project requirements into professional preliminary concepts faster.

Our Vision

Make architectural concept development faster, clearer and more accessible while keeping professional architectural review at the center of the final construction process.

Our Promise

We focus on practical space planning, circulation, privacy, ventilation, lighting and modern design principles.

How It Works

A simple workflow from client requirements to an AI-generated preliminary concept.

1

Enter Requirements

Choose plot size, dimensions, floors, bedrooms, bathrooms, kitchens, parking and style.

2

2D Floor Plan First

The layout is planned from the top with room placement, dimensions, doors, windows, stairs and parking.

3

3D Render From Same Layout

The realistic house view must follow the same floor-plan arrangement rather than inventing a different layout.

4

Professional Review

A licensed architect or engineer should review the concept before construction.
